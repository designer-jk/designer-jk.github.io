SafePay

Eliminating Social Media Commerce Scams via Fintech Escrow.

An end-to-end fintech mobile web solution that protects everyday social media buyers from advance-payment fraud using automated escrow-backed smart payment links, while giving independent Instagram/WhatsApp sellers a trust-verified checkout system.

Role

UX/UI Designer

About This Project

Social media commerce (C2C/SME) on platforms like Instagram and WhatsApp is booming. However, because these platforms lack native, secure transactional infrastructure, users must rely on blind trust. Buyers routinely send direct UPI payments to unverified sellers for highly advertised goods, only to be blocked, ghosted, or scammed. SafePay bridges this trust gap.

Platforms

Responsive Mobile Web (Buyer)

Native Mobile App (Seller)

Story

Imagine scrolling through Instagram and seeing an ad for a trendy hoodie at an unbeatable price of ₹299. You direct-message (DM) the page. The seller provides a UPI QR code. You pay instantly, expecting your order. Two weeks pass—no delivery, no tracking number, and your profile is blocked. You have just been scammed. SafePay rewrites this narrative: Instead of a raw QR code, the seller DMs you a SafePay escrow link. Your ₹299 is held securely by SafePay. The seller ships the item, you safely receive your hoodie, the escrow system verifies delivery, and the funds are released to the seller. No one gets scammed. Both sides win.

Problem Statement

How might we eliminate advance-payment fraud in social media commerce transactions without introducing heavy operational friction for individual, non-tech-savvy online sellers?

Solution

For Sellers
A rapid, 3-step link generation tool that tracks orders from dispatch to fund release.
For Buyers
A frictionless 1-page checkout that guarantees money-back security until the product physically arrives at their door.
