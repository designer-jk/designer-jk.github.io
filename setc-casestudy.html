TNSTC Digital Ecosystem

Digitizing Public Transit for 17 Million Daily Commuters.

A unified three-part digital solution—Customer App, Conductor POS App, and Admin Dashboard—designed to eliminate paper-based workflows, enable real-time tracking, and introduce universal accessibility to Tamil Nadu's state transit infrastructure.

Role

UX/UI Designer

About This Project

The Tamil Nadu State Transport Corporation (TNSTC) is one of India's largest public transport undertakings. This project introduces a modern, scalable digital layer to replace decades-old manual systems, improving transit equity for everyday citizens.

Platforms

Mobile (iOS & Android)

POS Terminal

Web Dashboard

Story

The project started with a simple question: Why does a state with peak smartphone penetration still use paper logbooks to track multi-ton vehicles? Navigating TNSTC today requires generational knowledge. Commuters wait blindly at stops, while conductors navigate high-stress, math-heavy environments inside moving buses. This case study details how we transformed systemic chaos into structured, human-centered digital touchpoints.

Problem Statement

The TNSTC relies on manual, paper-based workflows for drivers and conductors at timekeeper cabins, causing operational delays and data silos. Simultaneously, passengers lack real-time transit information, creating friction for diverse user groups including students, senior citizens, tourists, and the physically challenged.

Solution

Customer App
Live tracking, regional language audio-wayfinding, and digital ticketing.
Conductor POS App
Offers single-tap route logging, smart ticketing, and automated cash reconciliation.
Admin Panel
Delivers live fleet telemetry, automated timekeeper logs, and demand-forecasting insights.
