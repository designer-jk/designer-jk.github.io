Smart LPG Ecosystem

Smart LPG Management for Safer, Smarter Homes.

A connected LPG ecosystem designed to help households monitor gas levels, understand consumption, predict refill needs, and remotely manage safety—bringing greater visibility and control to everyday LPG usage.

Role

UX/UI Designer

About This Project

The Smart LPG Ecosystem is an integrated hardware-software solution designed to modernise domestic gas energy management in Indian households. By pairing a smart IoT-enabled regulator with an intuitive mobile app and an agency-facing web admin portal, the system eliminates the anxiety of sudden gas depletion, streamlines the booking process, and provides continuous automated safety monitoring against household gas leaks.

Platforms

Mobile (iOS & Android)

Web Dashboard

Story

In millions of Indian kitchens, cooking is the heartbeat of the home. However, managing the traditional LPG cylinder is an friction-filled experience shrouded in guesswork. Families routinely guess the remaining gas volume by lifting, shaking, or tapping the heavy steel cylinder. A sudden empty cylinder mid-meal causes domestic disruption, rushed frantic phone calls to the agency, and black-market delivery premiums. This project was born out of a desire to shift the relationship with domestic energy from an archaic, anxiety-inducing utility to a transparent, self-managing smart-home asset.

Problem Statement

Current Indian residential LPG infrastructure relies entirely on manual tracking, leading to unexpected gas depletion, inefficient booking cycles, and unmonitored safety risks. Consumers lack real-time visibility into their gas usage, making them vulnerable to unexpected run-outs, while regional gas agencies operate on reactive, un-optimized delivery models without predictive demand metrics.

Solution

Customer App
A mobile application that tracks real-time usage down to the exact percentage, predicts exact depletion dates, automates or prompts single-tap booking via UPI, and broadcasts instant smartphone alarms alongside automated valve shutoff during gas leaks.
For Agencies
A predictive B2B web dashboard showing real-time household depletion trends, allowing them to optimize stock supply, automate delivery dispatch logs, and proactively address localized safety warnings before they escalate.
